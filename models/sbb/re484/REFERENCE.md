# SBB Cargo Re 484 (Bombardier TRAXX F140 MS1): reference

Multi-system Bo'Bo' freight locomotive procured for Switzerland–Italy traffic (15 kV 16.7 Hz and
3 kV DC; the converter would also allow 25 kV and 1.5 kV DC). 21 units Re 484 001–021, built
2004–2007 (18 accepted in 2005, three more in 2007; 484 013–018 leased to Cisalpino 2005–2007).
Services from Bellinzona/Chiasso into Italy and, since 2019, the Lötschberg–Simplon corridor.
de.wikipedia reports SBB Cargo's plan to withdraw all 21 at the end of 2026 (not verified).

## Livery (ref01)
SBB Cargo: red (CC2) cab ends to just behind the cab doors, blue (CC1) body with large white
"cargo" lettering (suggested by letter-sized strokes), grey roof and a grey stripe along the
bottom of the sides, black windscreen surround, dark underframe and bogies.

Shape: TRAXX near-vertical front with a slightly raked two-pane windscreen; bogie centres ±5.2 m,
wheelbase 2.6 m (approximate). Builder: `parts/loco.py` (`LocoSpec`), livery repainted in the recipe.
