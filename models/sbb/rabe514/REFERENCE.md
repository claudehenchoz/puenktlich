# SBB RABe 514 "DTZ" (Siemens Desiro Double Deck): reference

61 four-car double-deck units RABe 514 001–061 for the S-Bahn Zürich, built 2005–2009 (Siemens;
bodies SKV Prague, middle cars fitted out by Stadler). Presented 2 December 2005, first service on
the S14 in 2006. Formation Bt-B-B-At; both end cars are motor cars with one pantograph each (no
15 kV roof line, so both pantographs are raised in service).

## Livery
The S-Bahn Zürich scheme (as the RABe 511 Regio-Dosto): white body, dark blue (CC2 dark shade)
skirt and waist line, red (CC1) front and door leaves, yellow panels over the 1st-class doors,
grey roof. Not modelled: the one-off "ZVV-Livery 2023" art wrap on 514 015.
