# SBB RABe 501 "Giruno" (Stadler SMILE / EC250): reference

11-car articulated low-floor high-speed unit for the Gotthard base tunnel, built by Stadler.
First passenger run 8 May 2019 (Zürich–Erstfeld). 41 units for SBB (501 001–041).

## Livery (colour map)
- Body white (CC2). Black window band with dark-framed windows; white band below with the
  SBB logo (red box, white cross) and "SBB CFF FFS" near one car end; black skirt.
- Red (CC1) cant-rail stripe along the whole train under the dark grey/black roof.
- Doors red (CC1) with a door window; a blue class panel ("1"/"2") next to each door;
  1st-class doors have a yellow bar above.
- Joints: black bellows covers, the car ends curve inwards.
- Nose (g03): black windscreen over most of the raked top; a red mask under it with the
  headlight clusters at its outer corners, grille and SBB logo; red bumper lip; white chin.
  On the side a red swoosh rises from the bumper along the lower windscreen edge to the
  cant-rail stripe at ~6.5 m; white below it.
- Restaurant car WR6 (g09): red band instead of black, "Restaurant" lettering in red above.
