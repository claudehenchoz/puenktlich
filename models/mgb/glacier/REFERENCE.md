# Glacier Express panorama coaches (Stadler, RhB + MGB): reference

The newest Glacier Express rolling stock: Stadler panorama coaches, ordered jointly by RhB and
MGB. Four complete trains came in 2006, eight more cars (2 Ap, 2 WRp, 4 Bp) for the 2009
season, and two replacements after 2010, 34 cars in all. In 2019 one Bp each of RhB and MGB
was converted into the Excellence Class car (Aps 1321, Aps 4046).

| Basic train | Ap–Api–WRp–Bp–Bp–Bp; HGe 4/4 II on MGB, Ge 4/4 III on RhB |

Sources: de.wikipedia "Glacier Express" and "Panoramawagen (Schweiz, Schmalspur)"; dimensions
from model-railway and enthusiast sites (rail-info.ch, drehscheibe-online.de,
modellbahnen.cadosch.org). Two sources give 3,695 mm and one 3,960 mm for the height; 3,695 mm
is used here. The WRp seat figure (10, bar) is a placeholder.

## Livery (colour map)
- Body pale light blue (fixed colour, ICY_BLUE). The car ends are red (CC1), with the boundary
  slanted so the red is wider at the bottom, and a slanted white stripe in each red end.
- A red skirt band along the bottom; black frames around the panorama windows and roof glazing.
- Class number ("1"/"2") in red next to the window block at the door end.
- WRp bar car: red all over, white slanted end stripes, "GLACIER EXPRESS" in large white letters.
- Excellence Class (g04): gold diagonal stripe between the red ends and the pale body,
  "EXCELLENCE CLASS" lettering in gold; the bar end has no gangway.
