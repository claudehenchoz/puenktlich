## Layout (metres behind the front tip)
- **Citaro**: wheelbase 5,845 mm; axles 2.7, 8.55; doors 0.5–1.85 (inward gliding),
  4.4–5.7 and 9.8–11.1 (outward swing-plug, the rear door behind the rear axle).
- **Citaro G** (C2 G drawing, 93.8 px/m; overhangs 2,805 / 3,430 mm, wheelbases 5,900 /
  5,990 mm): axles 2.85, 8.72, 14.7; joint 10.5; doors 0.55–1.94, 6.4–7.8, 12.4–13.8 and
  16.0–17.3 (VBZ layout 2-2-2-2: the fourth door behind the driven axle, estimated).
- Engine at the rear (no rear window); A/C pod on the roof.

## Data
OM 457 hLA: 220 kW (solo), 260 kW (G); 3.1 m high with A/C; 64 places (solo), 97 (G).
