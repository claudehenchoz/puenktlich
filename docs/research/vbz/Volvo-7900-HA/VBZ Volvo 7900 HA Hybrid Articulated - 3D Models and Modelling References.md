# VBZ Volvo 7900 HA Hybrid Articulated – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Volvo 7900 HA** is VBZ's second batch of Volvo hybrid articulated buses, delivered in **2020**. It is the **18.7 m length option** of the same Volvo 7900 Hybrid Articulated as the 2015–2018 batch. VBZ lists it at **18.74 × 2.55 × 3.30 m**, 29 t, Volvo D5K 240 diesel (177 kW) plus a 180 kW I-SAM electric motor (the 2017 buses have 150 kW), I-Shift (AT 2412F) gearbox. The VBZ page gives "61 places", which cannot be right for an 18.7 m bus; treat it as an error. It is part of the [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview|VBZ bus fleet]]. The shorter batch is [[VBZ Volvo 7900 LAH Hybrid Articulated - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 LAH]]; the 12 m plug-in sister is [[VBZ Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid]].

**Fleet numbers.** The [VBZ Hybridbusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/hybridbusse.html) says **14 units, "458 / 460–472"**, which leaves out 459. But **15 were delivered (458–472)**:
- VBZ annual report 2020: "15 additional diesel-hybrid articulated buses" went into service ([GB 2020](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2020.pdf)).
- A Flickr caption by The STB calls 459 (ZH 941459) part of "a series of 15 buses (458–472) put into service in 2020" ([Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/the_stb/55227003841), photo from Feb 2022).
- 459 was photographed in service on line 89 on 10 Apr 2023 (busbild-459).
- ÖV Panorama counted 34 classic hybrid articulated Volvos in Nov 2024 (19 + 15).
So the VBZ page now lists 14 and skips 459. Either 459 left the fleet after April 2023, or the page is wrong again (its LAH number field is also wrong). I found no report of an accident or withdrawal. A July 2026 snippet from [magyarbusz.info](https://magyarbusz.info/en/2026/07/29/zurich-electric-bus-tender-concluded-yutong-and-daimler-buses-replace-the-hess-man-pairing/) says VBZ runs "32 Volvo 7900A Hybrid", which would fit two withdrawals, but I could not open the page. **Unresolved.**

**Status (Nov 2026): in service.** Dated photos on bus-bild.de: 460 on line 70, 462 and 466 on line 69 (all 21 Mar 2026), 463, 469 and 471 in April 2025, 464 on trolleybus line 32 as a stand-in (July 2024). According to the GB 2020, ten diesel articulated buses were replaced by hybrid articulated buses that year. In the photos the HA mostly runs on lines 69, 70, 80 and 89, together with the LAH.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> **Same body generation as the LAH. I keep two notes because the length differs.**
> - **Length:** Volvo sells the 7900 Hybrid Articulated as **18 m** and **18.7 m**, each with 3 or 4 doors. All four Swiss rescue sheets say "Produktionsstart 2014", so it is one body generation. I overlaid the 18 m and 18.7 m side views from Volvo's sheets at the same scale, aligned at the front ([[#Blueprints and dimensions|overlay below]]). **Front overhang, front axle, front section, middle axle, articulation and the first rear-section door all coincide.** Only the **rear axle and rear end move back**, by roughly 0.6–0.7 m. So the HA has a longer rear section with a wider window bay between the rear door and the rear axle. The rear overhang stays about the same. This is a measurement from drawings, not a Volvo dimension.
> - **Face, doors, roof:** the same. Both have the classic 7900 front (black band with the diagonal Volvo bar, headlights in the lower corners), 4 doors (2+2+2+2), classic mirror arms, "HYBRID" lettering. Compare 447 (2017) with 463 (2020) in `VBZ-Volvo7900HA-compare-447-vs-463.jpg`.
> - **Destination display:** the HA has **white LED** displays. The LAH had **orange** ones as delivered, but some LAH (e.g. 455) now have white ones too. Use the fleet number (458–472) to be sure.
> - **Drivetrain:** 180 kW I-SAM instead of 150 kW. Not visible from outside.
> - **Bernmobil's 2022 series** (photo-05/06) is a Swiss look-alike from the same period with **camera mirrors** and white displays. Its length is not verified.
> - Not the same: **Volvo 7900 Electric Articulated** (battery bus, 18.7 m, no exhaust, roof battery pods), **Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid** (12 m), **Volvo BZL** (new chassis with third-party bodies).

> [!failure] No 3D model of the 18.7 m version (searched 2026-11-25)
> I found no model of the 18.7 m 7900 Hybrid Articulated in any format and nothing in VBZ livery.
> - **Closest free mesh:** [2014 Volvo 7900A Hybrid on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2014-volvo-7900a-hybrid-a2b47c32fc024f5ebca70f43306f6d04) (downloadable, CC BY, about 1.06 M faces, ATAM Italy livery, provenance unclear). It is the 18 m bus. To make the HA, cut the rear section behind the rear door and stretch it by about 0.6 m.
> - **Sketchfab** (search API: "volvo 7900", "volvo 7900 hybrid", "volvo 7900a", "volvo hybrid bus", "volvo articulated bus", "articulated bus", "gelenkbus", "vbz", "vbz bus", "zürich bus"): only that model plus a paid 7900 Electric (lazercar) and a 12 m game rip.
> - **Printables:** nothing. **MakerWorld:** nothing visible. **CGTrader, TurboSquid, Free3D, Cults3D:** bot walls, not verified.
> - **Games:** Cities: Skylines (Workshop "volvo 7900", "volvo hybrid", "VBZ", "Zürich"), Transport Fever 2 ("volvo 7900", "VBZ"), Cities: Skylines II (Paradox Mods "volvo 7900", "vbz", "zurich bus"), OMSI 2 (WebDisk category "7900(L)H (DLC)"): only 18 m models, no VBZ livery.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Build the 18 m bus first (see the LAH note), or start from the CC BY Sketchfab 7900A Hybrid.
> 2. Load `VBZ-Volvo7900HA-rescue-sheet-18.7m-4door-drawing.png`, scale it to 18,740 mm and line it up at the front with the 18 m drawing. Use the overlay image to see what moves.
> 3. Cut the rear section between the rear door and the rear axle and stretch it until the rear axle and the rear end match the 18.7 m sheet. Keep everything ahead of that cut.
> 4. Check the proportions against busbild-462 and busbild-461. Paint the VBZ livery as for the LAH, with a white LED destination display.
