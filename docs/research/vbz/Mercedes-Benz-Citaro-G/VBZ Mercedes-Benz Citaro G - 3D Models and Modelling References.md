# VBZ Mercedes-Benz Citaro G (O 530 G) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Mercedes-Benz Citaro G** (type code O 530 G) is the 18 m three-axle diesel articulated bus of the Verkehrsbetriebe Zürich. VBZ lists **31 units, fleet numbers 401–431**, built 2008/09, 2014 and 2015 ([VBZ – Autobusse](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/autobusse.html)). The series mixes two body generations. **401–424** (2008/09) are the **Citaro facelift** (first generation after the 2006 facelift). **425–431** (2014/15) are the **Citaro C2 G** (second generation). They came from a 2008 EvoBus tender that also covered the standard buses; the contract included a 14-year life-cycle-cost (LCC) maintenance deal ([Omnibusrevue, 29 Jan 2008](https://www.omnibusrevue.de/nachrichten/technik/verkehrsbetriebe-zuerich-vbz-erneuern-ihre-busflotte-2573595)). The solo version is in [[VBZ Mercedes-Benz Citaro - 3D Models and Modelling References|the VBZ Citaro note]]. The other diesel artic is in [[VBZ MAN Lion's City G - 3D Models and Modelling References|the MAN Lion's City G note]]. Fleet overview: [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]].

> [!info] Status in November 2026: all 31 in service
> - **busphoto.eu** ([VBZ vehicle list](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=13547), checked 2026-11-25) lists all of 401–431 as **"In operation"**, with no withdrawal dates. Recent dated photos there: 405 (14 Sep 2026), 431 (9 Sep 2026), 415 (8 Aug 2026), 412 (25 Jul 2026), 408/409/410/414 (8 Jul 2026), 418 (20 Jun 2026). Commons also has 412 in service on 21 Feb 2026 (photo-01).
> - An **older 2008 facelift-G batch** (ex 102, 107, 108, 110, 112, plates ZH 7451xx) was renumbered 185–189 in Dec 2021 and left VBZ in Sep 2023. Most went on to German operators (busphoto.eu, same list). Those are gone and are not part of 401–431.
> - **What's next:** in Aug 2026 the Zürich city council approved CHF 17 M for 8 battery standard buses (Yutong France) and **12 Mercedes-Benz eCitaro G** (Daimler Buses Schweiz), to enter service from mid-2027. VBZ replaces buses at the end of their technical life under its e-bus strategy ([busplaner, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.busplaner.de/de/news/zuerich-gruenes-licht-fuer-beschaffung-neuer-elektrobusse-349823.html)). No source says which Citaros the eCitaro G will replace. The 2008/09 batch is the obvious candidate.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Two generations in one series. Pick one before you model.**
>   - **Facelift (401–424):** the windscreen and roof edge are rounded. The headlights have a curved top edge and sit in a body-coloured front. The side window line is straight all the way to the rear. See photo-01, 02 and 03.
>   - **C2 G (425–431):** the front has a dark mask with a large star. The window line sweeps up in a "wave" at the rear end. See photo-09, 10, 11 and 12. It is also 185 mm longer (18,125 vs 17,940 mm).
> - **Doors:** VBZ buses have **4 doors** ("Türe 1" inward-gliding, "Türen 2–4" outward swing-plug, per VBZ). Many Commons photos show 3-door buses. Photo-05/06 are Japanese left-hand-traffic buses with the doors on the other side.
> - **Contractor look-alikes:** Limmat Bus and ATE Bus run Citaro G's for VBZ in full VBZ livery. Their fleet numbers are two-digit, e.g. "VBZ/Limmatbus Nr. 37 (Mercedes CitaroII O530G)" on [bus-bild.de](https://www.bus-bild.de/bild/schweiz~staedte~zurich/125097/vbzlimmatbus-nr-37-mercedes-citaroii-o530g.html). busphoto.eu files them under [Limmat Bus](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=22259) and [ATE Bus (operating for VBZ)](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=22179). Only 401–431 are VBZ's own.
> - **796** is an ex-BLT (Liestal 93) 2009 facelift G. busphoto.eu marks it as a loan bus ("Leihwagen MAN/Hess") since Dec 2024. It is not part of the 401–431 order.
> - **ETH Link:** the "ETH Zürich" shuttle buses in blue/white ETH livery seen on Commons are **eCitaro G run by Eurobus** ("Für die ETH Zürich fährt EUROBUS", [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ETH_Z%C3%BCrich_bus.jpeg)). They are not VBZ Citaro G's.
> - **eCitaro G** (from 2027): same C2 body family, but battery packs on the roof and a closed rear. It is a different model.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Decide on **facelift (401–424)** or **C2 G (425–431)**. Don't mix fronts and window lines.
> 2. Put `VBZ-CitaroG-C2G-4door-drawing.png` in Blender as a background. Scale it so the bus is 18,125 mm long. Set the axles at 2,805 mm from the front, then +5,900 mm and +5,990 mm. Use 2,550 mm width, 3,120 mm height with AC, and a 320 mm entrance height.
> 3. **C2 G:** model straight off the drawing. Check the front with photo-11 and the 3/4 views with photo-09/10, the rear with photo-12 and the interior with photo-13. If you own TF2, the free ICS Citaro C2 (G) is a good volume check.
> 4. **Facelift G:** keep the axle positions, make the body 17,940 mm, and redraw the front, roof edge and straight window line. Use photo-01/02/03 for the front and the left side, photo-04 for the door-side profile and photo-05/06 for the rear. The Japanese buses have their doors on the left, so mirror them. The OMSI 2 "Citybus O530 Facelift" (paid) has the exact 2-2-2-2 VBZ door layout to compare against.
> 5. VBZ livery: white body with a blue lower band and the "VBZ Züri-Linie" logo on the front (photo-01/02).
