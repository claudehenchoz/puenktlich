# VBZ MAN Lion's City G (Lion's City GL, A40, 510–515) – 3D Models and Modelling References

VBZ runs **six MAN Lion's City diesel articulated buses, fleet numbers 510–515**, delivered in December 2015. They are the previous-generation Lion's City (the body MAN built from 2004 until the 2018 redesign). The exact type is the **Lion's City GL**, chassis code **A40**, sales code **NG 363**: the long 18.75 m version, not the 18 m Lion's City G (A23). bus-bild.de captions them "MAN A40 Lion's City GL", German Wikipedia lists "MAN A40, 510–515, «Lion's City GL»", and ig-ikarus-200.de lists 510 (ZH 601510) as "MAN NG 363 Lion's City GL, 2015". The 18.75 m length on the VBZ data sheet confirms it. MAN gave the buses to VBZ free of charge as compensation for the rust problems of the 2003–2005 Neoplan Centroliners ([modellbus.info, 23 Dec 2015](http://www.modellbus.info/akt_15_12.htm)). They are a one-off series in a fleet of Mercedes, Volvo and (now) electric MAN buses. Part of [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]].

**Status (checked 2026-11-25): in service, all six.** German Wikipedia's VBZ fleet table (revision of [2026-11-02](https://de.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Verkehrsbetriebe_Z%C3%BCrich&oldid=271837047)) lists 510–515 as "total 6, heute 6" with no retirement date. A YouTube ride video of night line N3 dated 27 Oct 2026 names "MAN A40 Lion's City GL NG363, ZH 649514" (seen in the search snippet only; [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbnE-D4ulzM)). Another video shows 515 (ZH 708515) on line 304 on 1 June 2025 ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mcONlvj-g0)). The [VBZ Autobusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/autobusse.html) still lists the type, but that page is partly stale. It still shows the retired Centroliners.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Lion's City G (A23, 18 m) vs GL (A40, 18.75 m).** The bodies look the same. MAN's 2013 brochure gives the G a front wheelbase of 5,105 mm and the GL 5,875 mm. The rear wheelbase is 6,770 mm on both. So the extra 0.77 m sits in the **front section**, between door 2 and the front axle. Compare the two MAN sketches below. Most game assets are the 18 m G.
> - **Doors.** The VBZ buses have **3 doors**: door 1 is an inward-gliding double door, doors 2 and 3 are outward-swinging plug doors (VBZ data sheet). Door 3 sits in the rear section. Many GLs elsewhere have 4 or 5 doors (the CNG "Bus of the Year 2015" demonstrator has 5; photo-04).
> - **CNG versions** (Copenhagen, Vicenza, the IAA 2012 bus) carry a large gas-tank fairing along the roof (photo-04, 09–11). The VBZ buses are diesel (MAN D2066 LUH52, Euro 6) and have only the A/C pods.
> - **2018+ MAN Lion's City 18 / 18 E** (new generation): angular front, black window band, totally different body. VBZ runs the 18 E as 500–503 and 474–494; see [[VBZ MAN Lion's City 18 E - 3D Models and Modelling References]]. Most "MAN Lion's City" assets on Sketchfab and Steam are this new generation.
> - **Contractor buses** run for VBZ by ATE Bus and Limmat Bus are out of scope. A 2023 Instagram post (search snippet only, not verified) says ATE Bus runs a new-generation Lion's City as a VBZ subcontractor.
> - **Neoplan Centroliner N 4522** (VBZ 516–562, retired by 2023) shares MAN's platform era but has a different body. **Mercedes Citaro G** is the other VBZ diesel artic: [[VBZ Mercedes-Benz Citaro G - 3D Models and Modelling References]].
> - **Livery** (from bus-bild.de photo of 513): white body, VBZ blue lower band, black glazing band, white roof. The closest Commons livery is Lausanne TL (photo-03, 05–07).

> - **Sketchfab** (search API; queries "man lion's city", "lions city", "lion's city g", "lion's city gl", "lions city g", "man lion city articulated", "man a23", "man a40", "man ng 363", "man a21", "man nl 283", "man nl 263", "man ng", "man articulated bus", "articulated bus", "gelenkbus", "bendy bus", "man city bus", "man bus", "man lion", "omsi", "vbz bus", "zürich bus", "zurich bus"): **no previous-generation articulated Lion's City.** The MAN hits are new-generation 12 m buses (karpworkz "Lion's City 12G EfficiencyHybrid", CC BY, downloadable; arman3dg, Haapis, anhtu14072004, view-only) and two view-only A21 rigid buses (chesterjackson, john4er).

### OMSI 2 (freeware)
- [NEOMAN Overhaul V3](https://reboot.omsi-webdisk.de/file/14410-neoman-overhaul-v3/) by Sobol (OMSI-WebDisk, Dec 2025, 93k downloads). This is a free rebuild of the whole Lion's City family. It needs no payware add-on. It includes the **A23 G (18 m)** with door layouts 2-2-2-0 and 2-2-2-2, configurable for any build year from 2004 to 2023 (so Euro 6 too). It has no GL. Licence: CC BY-NC, **for use only inside OMSI 2**. Conversion or extraction outside OMSI 2 is explicitly prohibited. So use it as a visual check in the game, not as a mesh source.
- **VBZ repaints:** none for the old Lion's City. OMSI-WebDisk has VBZ repaints for the Citaro G facelift and the new-generation MAN Lion's City 12C (Captain Zweistein), which are other models.
- Transport Fever 2: I found no old-generation Lion's City G/GL in the Workshop (queries "lion's city", "MAN NG", "MAN A23", "MAN A40" etc.). The popular "MAN Lion's City (C)" mod by WTrainy12 is the new generation.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Put `VBZ-MANLCG-MAN-GL-side-sketch.png` in Blender as a background and scale it to 18.75 m. Check the axles against 5,875 mm (front axle to middle axle) and 6,770 mm (middle axle to rear axle). Use 2,500 mm width and about 2,880 mm body height (2.98 m with roof units per VBZ).
> 2. The sketch shows the door side with the same 3-door layout as VBZ: door 1 at the front, door 2 just ahead of the middle axle, door 3 just ahead of the rear axle. Check it against the bus-bild.de photo of 513. Take the left side from photo-01/02.
> 3. Block out the front from photo-07/08 (head-on) and photo-05/06/09 (3/4). Use photo-10/13 for the rear and photo-01 for the bellows and the joint.
> 4. If you own OMSI 2, load NEOMAN Overhaul V3 (A23, Euro 6 setting) to check volumes by eye. Stretch the front section by 0.77 m for the GL.
> 5. Livery: white, VBZ blue lower band, black window band. Take the colours from the bus-bild.de photo of 513.
