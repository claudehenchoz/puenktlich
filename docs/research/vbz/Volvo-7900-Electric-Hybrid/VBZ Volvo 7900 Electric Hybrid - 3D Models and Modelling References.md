# VBZ Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid** is a 12 m, two-axle plug-in hybrid city bus. It has the same parallel hybrid drive as the 7900 Hybrid (Volvo D5K 240 diesel, 177 kW, plus a 150 kW I-SAM electric motor) but a much bigger lithium-ion battery that can be charged in the depot and at the roadside through an **OppCharge** pantograph. VBZ has **16 of them, fleet numbers 620–635**. VBZ lists them at **12.13 × 2.55 × 3.52 m**, 19 t, 69 places. Milani Design did the interior. They belong to the [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview|VBZ bus fleet]]; the articulated hybrids are in [[VBZ Volvo 7900 LAH Hybrid Articulated - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 LAH]] and [[VBZ Volvo 7900 HA Hybrid Articulated - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 HA]].

**History.** VBZ chose the Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid for a 16-bus tender in 2018. 14 were delivered in 2019 and 2 in 2020 (VBZ annual reports [2018](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2018.pdf) and [2019](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2019.pdf)). Fleet numbers 620–635 come from the [VBZ Hybridbusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/hybridbusse.html) and [6-tram.ch](https://6-tram.ch/rund-um-die-vbz/fahrzeuge/standardbusse/), and match the fan photos (621, 623, 625, 626, 629, 631, 634).

**Charging and lines.** For the first years the buses were charged only in the depot. VBZ planned a roadside charger at the **Dunkelhölzli** terminus of line 67 from 2020 on ([GB 2019](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2019.pdf), [GB 2021](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2021.pdf)). It opened in **August 2022** ([VBZ Fakten 2025, chronicle](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/vbz/de/die-vbz/zahlen-fakten/geschaeftsbericht/Z4690_VBZ_FB-2025_Web.pdf)). VBZ said the charger would let the buses run about 70 % of their duty purely electric ([GB 2020](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2020.pdf); that report says "line 69", probably a slip, since GB 2021 and ÖV Panorama both say line 67). [ÖV Panorama (Nov 2024)](https://www.ovpanorama.ch/index.php/2024/11/12/drei-jahrzehnte-polnische-schweden/) says the buses run on the linked **lines 67 and 76** and charge at Dunkelhölzli. Fan photos show them on **67** (Dunkelhölzli / Bahnhof Wiedikon, 2025–2026), **78** (to Dunkelhölzli, 2021–2023) and **66** (Kilchberg, 18 Nov 2026). I found no source for the make of the Dunkelhölzli charging mast.

**Status (Nov 2026): all 16 in service** as far as I can tell. The newest dated photo is 629 on line 66 on 18 Nov 2026 ([bus-bild.de](https://www.bus-bild.de/bild/schweiz~staedte~zurich/228300/vbz---volvo-7900-hybrid-nr629.html)). An Instagram post from Dec 2025 says about 50 Volvos run in and around Zürich, including 16 Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrids from 2019 (seen only as a search snippet of [this post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DR2gZZYDByy/)). 50 = 34 articulated hybrids + 16 Electric Hybrids, which fits.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Volvo 7900 Hybrid (12 m):** same body. The Electric Hybrid adds the **charging-rail pod on the front of the roof** (the slotted dome above the windscreen, see busbild-621 and photo-07), "ELECTRIC HYBRID" lettering instead of "HYBRID", and a bigger battery. VBZ's height of 3.52 m (against 3.28 m for the plain hybrid) probably includes the roof rails. Swiss 7900 Hybrid users on Commons: PostAuto (Thurgau) and Stadtbus Kreuzlingen. I could not verify 7900 Hybrids at Stadtbus Winterthur or BVB Basel; VBL Luzern has the articulated 7900A Hybrid.
> - **Volvo 7900 Electric** (full battery bus): same body family, no diesel engine, so the rear end and roof equipment differ. The only ones in Switzerland are four at Travys in Yverdon-les-Bains (2022, per ÖV Panorama).
> - **Volvo BZL:** a newer electric chassis with bodies from MCV and others. Different shape.
> - **VBZ batch differences:** I saw none between 620–635. The 2019 delivery has white LED displays, classic mirror arms and the VBZ "Züri-Linie" logo.

> [!failure] No free, downloadable 3D model of the 7900 Electric Hybrid (searched 2026-11-25)
> - **Sketchfab** (search API: "volvo 7900", "volvo 7900 electric", "volvo 7900 electric hybrid", "volvo electric bus", "volvo hybrid bus", "volvo city bus", "oppcharge", "vbz", "zürich bus"): no Electric Hybrid. Closest: [Volvo 7900 Electric Bus by lazercar](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/volvo-7900-electric-bus-5a343a0b4351456a9ce5783793bf55ad) (2.2 M faces, view only; sold as .blend/FBX on the author's Gumroad, price not checked). It is the full-electric 7900 in Göteborg ElectriCity livery, so the roof and rear need changes. *Volvo 7900 - Bus Simulator 17* (vj32621, 13k faces, CC BY claimed) is a re-upload of a mobile-game asset; I would not trust the licence.
> - **Printables** ("volvo 7900", "volvo 7900 electric", "volvo hybrid bus", "vbz bus"): nothing. **MakerWorld:** nothing visible. **CGTrader, TurboSquid, Free3D, Cults3D:** bot walls, not verified.
> - **No VBZ-livery asset** in Cities: Skylines (Workshop "VBZ"), Transport Fever 2, Cities: Skylines II (Paradox Mods) or OMSI 2.

## Free game assets (closest thing to a download)

### Cities: Skylines (Steam Workshop): D3S Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid
Free, by DANZ / D3S Designs, 2018. 12 m, 2 axles, 3 doors, capacity 70, "applies the color line" (Steam description). The preview shows the raised front roof pod with charging rails. You need Cities: Skylines (app 255710); the mesh is in a `.crp` file. This is the best match: a purpose-made Electric Hybrid with the roof pod. No VBZ repaint exists, but the base version takes your line colour.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Load `VBZ-Volvo7900EH-Volvo-brochure-2015-drawing-12134.png` in Blender and scale the plan to 12,134 mm. Use 2,550 mm width and 3,280 mm body height; the roof pod brings it to about 3.52 m.
> 2. Block out the body from the drawing. Check the axle positions against the overhang figures (2,664 / 5,945 / 3,435 mm) and the rescue-sheet drawing. If you own Cities: Skylines, open the D3S Electric Hybrid to check volumes.
> 3. Front: photo-03, photo-04 and busbild-626/629. Rear: photo-05 and photo-12. Roof pod and rails: busbild-621, photo-06, photo-07 and photo-09.
> 4. Interior from the Milani images: taupe ELeather seats, grey shells, lime-green accents, stainless grab poles. Cab from photo-11.
> 5. VBZ livery: white with a blue lower band, black panel at the front door with "ELECTRIC HYBRID" lettering, white LED destination display.
