# VBZ Hess Swisstrolley 3 (BGT-N2C, 144–161) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Hess Swisstrolley 3** is the third generation of the Hess low-floor articulated trolleybus. The exact type is Hess / Vossloh Kiepe **BGT-N2C** (B = bus, G = articulated, T = trolley, N = low floor, 2 = two driven axles). VBZ bought **18 of them, fleet numbers 144–161**, built 2006–2007. They replaced the first series of Mercedes O 405 GTZ trolleybuses. The VBZ cars have the **long body (18.7 m)**, a **single-leaf front door** and the **CO-BOLT 3 design** that Hess introduced in 2006. Chassis and body are by Carrosserie Hess (Bellach), the 600 V electrics by Vossloh Kiepe, and from the Zürich batch onward the motors came from Traktionssysteme Austria (TSA). Sources: [VBZ Trolleybusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/trolleybusse.html) and [Wikipedia (DE) – Hess Swisstrolley](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess_Swisstrolley#Swisstrolley_3). This note is part of [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The successor in Zürich is the [[VBZ Hess Swisstrolley 4 - 3D Models and Modelling References|Swisstrolley 4 (162–182)]]. The double-articulated sister is the [[VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus - 3D Models and Modelling References|lighTram 3]], and the replacement is the [[VBZ Hess lighTram 19 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References|lighTram 19 DC]].

> [!important] Fleet status (checked 2026-11-25): 8 of 18 still in service
> - **In service:** 145, 146, 149, 150, 152, 158, 160, 161.
> - **Withdrawn (10):** 144 (out of service 31.12.2024 after a collision at Rosengartenstrasse, left 2025), 148 (out 12.2024), 154 and 155 (left and scrapped 12.2024), 147, 151, 153, 156, 157 and 159 (all 2025). Source: the community roster on [transphoto.org – VBZ trolleybuses](https://transphoto.org/list.php?did=408). It gives no onward destination.
> - **Cross-check:** [VBZ Fakten 2025 (PDF)](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/vbz/de/die-vbz/zahlen-fakten/geschaeftsbericht/Z4690_VBZ_FB-2025_Web.pdf) gives **53 articulated trolleybuses** at the end of 2025. That is exactly 8 ST3 + 21 ST4 + SwissTrolley plus 183 + 23 lighTram 19 DC (200–222). [Wikipedia (DE) – Trolleybus Zürich](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybus_Z%C3%BCrich) says that from 2025 "various" Swisstrolley 3 and lighTram 3 were replaced by new lighTrams and withdrawn. Its fleet table still lists all 18, and the VBZ vehicle page also still says 18. Both are out of date.
> - **Outlook:** transphoto lists lighTram 19 DC 223–231 as delivered in 2026 and 232–236 as further entries without dates. That suggests the last eight ST3 will go soon, but I found no official retirement date.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Swisstrolley 4 (VBZ 162–182, BGT-N2D, 2013)** uses the same CO-BOLT 3 body and front. In photos the quickest tell is the **fleet number**. The second tell is the **destination display**: the ST3 has a **green-yellow** display (photo-01, 02, 04, 05), the ST4 an **orange LED** display. See the side-by-side crop in the [[VBZ Hess Swisstrolley 4 - 3D Models and Modelling References|ST4 note]]. Under the skin the ST4 has ZF independent front suspension, stronger drive axles and 2 × 120 kW instead of 2 × 160 kW. Both have Kiepe electrics.
> - **Older CO-BOLT 2 Swisstrolley 3** (Geneva TPG 731–768, Lucerne VBL 201–210, built 2004–2006) have the rounder, Scania-like front with a separate destination box. The free 3D Warehouse model below is one of these.
> - **Other long-body CO-BOLT 3 Swisstrolley 3** look almost identical: Lausanne TL 831–865 and Neuchâtel TransN 131–150 (both with a single-leaf front door like VBZ), Winterthur SBW 101–124 (single-leaf front door, only axle 3 driven), St. Gallen VBSG 171–187, Lucerne VBL 211–226, Fribourg TPF 522–533 and Schaffhausen VBSH 101–107. **Biel 51–60** have the **short body** (17.93 m). Solingen and Arnhem cars are short three-door versions. Source: Wikipedia (DE) delivery table.
> - **Bern** never had a Swisstrolley 3. Its Swisstrolley 2 fleet went to Sarajevo in 2019–2020, and it now runs Swisstrolley 5.
> - **lighTram 19 DC (VBZ 200–236)** and **SwissTrolley plus (183)** are the fifth generation, with a new front and a battery on the roof. Careful: Hess and VBZ also call the lighTram 19 DC "BGT-N2D", the same code as the ST4.
> - **Technical detail that changed:** Wikipedia lists a diesel generator (50/80 kW) as the ST3 auxiliary unit. Today's VBZ page lists a **120 kW lithium-titanate traction battery** for emergency running. A 2011 Hess press clipping says the 2012/13 order was the first VBZ trolleybus order with an electric store (see the ST4 note). So the battery is probably a later retrofit. I found no source for when it happened.

## Free game assets (closest thing to a download)

### Transport Fever 2 / Transport Fever / Train Fever: Seamon's Hess Swisstrolley
This is the only purpose-built CO-BOLT 3 Swisstrolley I found. Seamon says the pack has **all versions of the Swisstrolley 3 and 4 with this look**, plus the lighTram. The versions differ in length (18, 18.75 or 24.25 m), number of double doors (3, 4 or 5), first door (single or double), A/C unit size and trolley poles. The TF2 version has 3 LODs, animated doors and wipers, an interior with passengers and driver, and its own sound set. Terms: repaints that need the original are free; stand-alone repaints and ports to other games need Seamon's consent, and default to CC BY-NC after 6 months without an answer. The meshes are in game formats and need the game.

## Blueprints and dimensions

I found **no type sketch** (Typenskizze) or dimension drawing for the Swisstrolley 3. The current Hess site has none, and the old VBZ ST3 page is not in the Wayback Machine. For scale, use the overall dimensions below and the photos.

| Item | VBZ Swisstrolley 3 (144–161) |
|---|---|
| Type | Hess / Vossloh Kiepe BGT-N2C, 3 axles, axles 2 and 3 driven, 100 % low floor |
| Length / width / height | 18.7 m / 2.55 m / 3.45 m (VBZ). Wikipedia: long body 18.67 m, height 3.44 m |
| Kerb weight | 19.5 t |
| Power | 2 × 160 kW three-phase induction motors (TSA), 600 V |
| Top speed | 60 km/h |
| Emergency drive | today 120 kW lithium-titanate traction battery (VBZ). Originally a diesel generator, per Wikipedia's type table (50/80 kW) |
| Doors | door 1: single-leaf inward-swinging sliding door; doors 2–4: outward-swinging sliding (plug) doors |
| Accessibility | wheelchair space and fold-out ramp at door 2, kneeling |
| Climate | full air-conditioning |
| Built / delivered | 2006–2007 (144 arrived 19.07.2006, 160 on 19.10.2007, per transphoto) |

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Start from the free geometry: import `VBZ-Swisstrolley3-3DWarehouse-trainboy2004-Luzern-ST3.glb` for the body volume, window rhythm, articulation and roof layout. Rescale it to 18.7 m × 2.55 m × 3.45 m. Its scale and origin are SketchUp exports, so check them.
> 2. Replace the front with the CO-BOLT 3 front from the OMSI WebDisk lighTram 3 Blender source (St. Gallen version), or model it from photo-02 and photo-05 (head-on) and photo-01 and photo-04 (3/4). Change the front door to a **single leaf**.
> 3. Set the side window and door positions from photo-08 (offside) and photo-03 (door side), scaled to 18.7 m. Use photo-10 for the roof and photo-09 for the rear.
> 4. Livery: VBZ white with a blue skirt, a black window band and a thin blue line along the top of the side windows, plus a green-yellow destination display. Pick a fleet number from the eight still in service (145, 146, 149, 150, 152, 158, 160, 161) if you want a 2026-correct bus.
