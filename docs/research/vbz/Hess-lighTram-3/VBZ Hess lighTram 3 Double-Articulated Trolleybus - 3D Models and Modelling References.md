# VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus (BGGT-N2C / BGGT-N2D, fleet 61–91) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Hess lighTram 3** is a 24.7 m, four-axle, double-articulated low-floor trolleybus (Hess type **BGGT-N2C**). VBZ calls it the *Doppelgelenk-Trolleybus* (DGT). Hess and the press also used the names *Longo* and *Megatrolley*. The body and chassis are by Carrosserie Hess in Bellach, the 600 V electrics by **Vossloh Kiepe**. VBZ runs two series. **61–77** (17 buses) arrived in 2007/08 for line 31, after two loans from Geneva in 2004 and 2006. The first entered service on 11 September 2007. **78–91** (14 buses, type **BGGT-N2D**, called "Doppelgelenk-Trolleybus 2" or *Longo 2* by VBZ and *lighTram 4* by German Wikipedia) followed in 2012 (78–89) and 2014 (90–91), so that line 32 could also go double-articulated. They run on lines 31 and 32. Axles 2 and 3 are driven and axles 1 and 4 steer. This note belongs to [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The newer double-articulated buses are in [[VBZ Hess lighTram 25 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References]], and the articulated battery trolleybus is in [[VBZ Hess lighTram 19 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References]].

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **61–77 vs 78–91:** the two VBZ series look almost the same. VBZ says the Zürich buses have their **own front and rear design** and a **single-leaf front door** (VBZ press release, 16 Aug 2007, saved below). The 78–91 have a new chassis design, a battery instead of the diesel auxiliary unit, and LED instead of LCD destination displays (VBZ trolleybus page). I could not find a clear visual difference in the photos. Check the fleet number before you copy a detail: photo-06 (63) and photo-07 (72) are first series, photo-08 (80), photo-09 and photo-11 (91) and the VBZ photo of 90 are second series.
> - **Other lighTram 3 operators:** Geneva TPG 781–790 (2005–06) and Lucerne VBL 231–233 (2006) have the older **CO-BOLT 2** front and a **double-leaf front door**. St. Gallen VBSG 188–194 (2009) also has a double-leaf front door. Lucerne's lighTram 4 (VBL 234–242 and 401–417) has a **tram-like front**. Source: [Wikipedia (DE) – Hess lighTram Trolley](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess_lighTram_Trolley).
> - **VBZ 92–94** (2018, "Doppelgelenk-Trolleybus plus") and **VBZ 1–13** (2025/26, lighTram 25 DC) are different, newer bodies. See [[VBZ Hess lighTram 25 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References]].
> - **Prototypes:** Geneva 721 (lighTram 1, a lengthened Swisstrolley 1, 2003–2016) and St. Gallen 155 (BGGT 5-25, a high-floor NAW bus with a low-floor trailer section). Neither is a lighTram 3.
> - **Hess lighTram Hybrid** (diesel-electric, e.g. Luxembourg Emile Weber, Qbuzz) shares the body family but has no poles.
> - **Refit:** 6-tram.ch says the first series had a diesel auxiliary unit at first and a battery pack since 2017.
> - **Livery:** all are VBZ white and blue. Many buses carry side adverts (photo-01, 02 and 14: "Letzipark express"; photo-07: "NBW").

> [!info] Fleet status (checked 2026-11-25)
> - The [Wikipedia (DE) fleet table](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybus_Z%C3%BCrich) still lists **61–77 (17)** and **78–91 (14)**. The same article says, without a source or numbers: "from 2025 several Swisstrolley 3 and lighTram 3 were replaced by new lighTrams and retired."
> - [VBZ Fakten 2025 (PDF)](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/vbz/de/die-vbz/zahlen-fakten/geschaeftsbericht/Z4690_VBZ_FB-2025_Web.pdf) gives **35 double-articulated trolleybuses** at the end of 2025. In 2018 VBZ had 34 ([vbzonline.ch](https://vbzonline.ch/happy-birthday-trolleybus/)). So the new 1–13 had only partly arrived and/or some old buses had already gone. The PDF does not break this down.
> - [Wikipedia (EN)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybuses_in_Zurich) says delivery of the new double-articulated buses (from no. 1) began in mid-2025 (citing *Trolleybus Magazine* 384).
> - When Hess won the order, VBZ said the new buses were for new electric lines but "in time also to replace the current fleet" ([Urban Transport Magazine, 17 Nov 2022](https://www.urban-transport-magazine.com/grossauftrag-fuer-hess-lightram-gehen-nach-zuerich/)).
> - Line 69 only went over to trolleybuses on 19 October 2026 because further buses arrived late (Wikipedia DE).
> - **My reading:** 78–91 are in service. 61–77 are probably **partly retired** since 2025, but I found no list of which numbers. Treat any single bus as unconfirmed. The [VBZ trolleybus page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/trolleybusse.html) still lists both series and is partly out of date.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Put `VBZ-lighTram3-drawing-side-left.png` and `-side-right.png` in Blender as side backgrounds and scale them to **24,660 mm**. Place the axles at 0, 5,845, 12,543 and 19,253 mm from axle 1. Put the plan view under them. Use 2,550 × 3,440 mm and the front/rear crop for the cross-section.
> 2. Block out the three body sections and the two bellows first. Then cut the doors from the door-side view: 1 single-leaf door at the front and 4 double doors.
> 3. Model the front from photo-06, 07 and 08 and the VBZ photo of 75. Model the rear from photo-10 and 11. Take the roof housings from the drawing and photo-12.
> 4. If you own Transport Fever 2, load Seamon's lighTram (5 doors, single front door) to check volumes. Its in-game length is 24.25 m, so scale it to the drawing.
> 5. Pick a series (61–77 or 78–91) and one advert or plain livery before texturing.
