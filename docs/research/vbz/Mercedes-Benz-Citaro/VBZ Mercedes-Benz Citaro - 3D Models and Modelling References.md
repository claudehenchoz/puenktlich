# VBZ Mercedes-Benz Citaro (O 530 facelift, 12 m) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Mercedes-Benz Citaro** (type code O 530) is the 12 m two-axle diesel standard bus of the Verkehrsbetriebe Zürich. All of VBZ's are the **first-generation facelift** body, built 2008/09. VBZ's page lists fleet numbers **601, 604–607, 612 and 614–617**. That is 10 numbers, although the same table says "Stückzahl 8" ([VBZ – Autobusse](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/autobusse.html)). They came from the same 2008 EvoBus order as the articulated buses, which included a 14-year life-cycle-cost maintenance deal ([Omnibusrevue, 29 Jan 2008](https://www.omnibusrevue.de/nachrichten/technik/verkehrsbetriebe-zuerich-vbz-erneuern-ihre-busflotte-2573595)). The articulated sister is in [[VBZ Mercedes-Benz Citaro G - 3D Models and Modelling References|the VBZ Citaro G note]]. Fleet overview: [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]].

> [!info] Status in November 2026: 10 of the original 19 still in service
> - The series was originally **601–619** (plates ZH 745601–745619). busphoto.eu ([VBZ vehicle list](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=13547), checked 2026-11-25) shows **601, 604–607, 612 and 614–617 "In operation"**. That matches the VBZ page.
> - **Withdrawn** (busphoto.eu): 603 (2017/18), 609 (Apr 2023), 613 (May 2023), 610 (2023), 608 (Jul 2023), 602 (Sep 2023), 611 (out of service since May 2024) and 618 (May 2024). **619** became driving-school bus 4281 in Jul 2024.
> - **Recent dated photos** of the survivors on busphoto.eu: 612 (4 Oct 2026), 614 (8 Aug 2026), 616 (4 May 2026), 605 (11 Jan 2026), 606 (17 Dec 2025). So at least part of the series ran in autumn 2026.
> - **Outlook:** the 2026 e-bus order (8 battery standard buses from Yutong France, 12 eCitaro G, in service from mid-2027) replaces buses at the end of their technical life ([busplaner, 2 Aug 2026](https://www.busplaner.de/de/news/zuerich-gruenes-licht-fuer-beschaffung-neuer-elektrobusse-349823.html)). The remaining 17–18-year-old Citaros are likely candidates, but no source names them.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Generation:** VBZ's buses are the **facelift** (2006–2011): curved-top headlights, a body-coloured front and a straight window line. The **first generation before the facelift** (1998–2005, flat front, plainer lamps) and the **Citaro C2** (from 2011, dark front mask, wavy window line) are different bodies. Many game assets are one of those two (see below).
> - **Doors:** VBZ: **3 doors**, door 1 inward-gliding, doors 2–3 outward swing-plug, 2 wheelchair places, manual ramp at door 2 (VBZ page).
> - **Contractor look-alikes:** Limmat Bus and ATE Bus run Citaros in VBZ livery with two-digit fleet numbers (busphoto.eu: [Limmat Bus](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=22259), [ATE Bus for VBZ](https://busphoto.eu/list.php?did=22179)). Only 601–619 were VBZ's own.
> - **Driving-school Citaros:** 4264/4265 (ex 4068/4069) and 4281 (ex 619) are in VBZ's driving school, not in passenger service.
> - **Hybrid look-alikes:** VBZ's 12 m Volvo 7900 hybrids (620–635) share the white/blue livery, not the body.

> [!failure] No free, general-purpose 3D model of the facelift Citaro (searched 2026-11-25)
> There is no free, scale-accurate OBJ, FBX, glTF, .blend or STL of a **facelift** Citaro.
> - **Sketchfab** (search API; same queries as the Citaro G note: "citaro", "citaro facelift", "o530", "o 530", "mercedes citaro", "mercedes-benz citaro", "citaro hybrid", "vbz", "zürich bus" …) found these:

>   - a free facelift-style low-poly solo: zairiq, "1997 Citaro C Hybrid", CC BY, 4,144 faces, roof hybrid modules;
>   - a [Low Poly Mercedes-Benz Citaro 530](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/881e83d1378c496eb8e42708a4ad3b96) (CC BY, 49k tris). I could not tell its generation from the thumbnail.
> - **Printables:** only a [1:14 Citaro](https://www.printables.com/model/145781-mercedes-citaro-114) (generation not checked) and Citaro cab parts.
> - Not checked, because search engines were down: CGTrader, TurboSquid, Cults3D, omsi-webdisk.de, transportfever.net, Paradox Mods.

> [!note] No facelift drawing found
> I found no 2006–2011 Daimler/EvoBus data sheet with a type drawing. The Wayback index of mercedes-benz-bus.com starts around 2018, and evobus.com has no archived Citaro PDFs. The C2 brochure saved in the Citaro G note (`VBZ-CitaroG-Daimler-TI-Citaro-Stadtbusse-2019-DE.pdf`, page 3) has vector drawings of the **C2** solo. Its body is longer (12,135 mm, 5,900 mm wheelbase), so use it only as a guide to proportions. Build the facelift from the dimensions above and the photos.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Block out an 11,950 × 2,550 mm box with a 5,845 mm wheelbase. Make it 2,869 mm high without the AC and about 3.1 m with it.
> 2. Model the front from photo-01 (VBZ 608) and photo-02, the sides from photo-03/04, the rear from photo-05/06 and the roof from photo-07.
> 3. Put the 3 double doors in VBZ's layout (front, middle, rear). Check the positions against bus-bild.de's VBZ 607 photo or the OMSI 2 "Citybus O530 Facelift" 2-2-2 version.
> 4. If you model the Citaro G first, reuse its facelift front, rear and roof pods. Both share the facelift styling.
