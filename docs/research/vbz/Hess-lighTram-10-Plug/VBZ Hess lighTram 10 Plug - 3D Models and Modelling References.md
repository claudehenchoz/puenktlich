# VBZ Hess lighTram 10 Plug (Batterie-Quartierbus 340–354) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Hess lighTram 10 Plug** is the smallest battery bus in Carrosserie Hess's lighTram family. It is a two-axle, two-door midibus with an aluminium body bolted together in Hess's CO-BOLT system, and all batteries sit on the roof. It charges only in the depot, through a CCS2 plug. VBZ runs **15 of them as fleet numbers 340–354**: 340–347 came in 2022 and 348–354 in 2023. VBZ calls them *Batterie-Quartierbusse* and uses them on the narrow, hilly district lines (Quartierbuslinien) out of the Hardau garage. VBZ lists them at 10.80 × 2.55 × 3.30 m, with a Siemens motor and 100 kW CCS2 depot charging. The order was first meant to be a CaetanoBus vehicle that Hess would import. Hess then built the bus itself when Caetano changed its range ([tir-transnews.ch, Apr 2021](https://www.tir-transnews.ch/bus/zuercher-quartier-batteriebusse-kommen-neu-von-hess/)). The fleet overview is in [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]].

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Swiss eBus plus (VBZ no. 184)** is a 16th lighTram 10 Plug. It is a Hess demonstrator built in a research project funded by the Swiss Federal Office of Energy (BFE), with ETH Zürich and the Bern University of Applied Sciences (BFH). It has been running for VBZ on the Quartierbus lines since September 2024, and the project runs until 2026. It is **not** part of 340–354. It has a 510 kWh battery instead of 333 kWh, 350 kW CCS charging, CO₂ heat pumps and better insulation. Hess gives its capacity as 76 + 2 ([Hess factsheet, April 2024](http://9393058.hs-sites.com/de-ch/projekt-swissebusplus); [Hess press release, June 2024](https://www.hess-ag.ch/unternehmen/news/news-detail/news/detail/News/swiss-ebus-plus-der-neue-batterie-elektrobus.html?L=0&cHash=56c186de78e3e6a932344428579a2189)). The body is the same, but you can tell it apart in photos. The roof dome over the windscreen is **blue** (it is white on 340–354), and it has "Swiss eBus plus" lettering behind the front door. This is the bus Hess showed on its stand at **Busworld Europe 2025** in Brussels ([tir-transnews.ch, Nov 2025](https://www.tir-transnews.ch/bus/spannende-neuheiten-an-der-busworld-europe-2025/)). All three Commons photos of the type from the show are of no. 184 (photo-02, 03).
> - **Hess lighTram 12 Plug** is the 12 m two-axle version. It is 3.5 m tall instead of 3.3 m and has a standing area in the rear and usually three doors (Hess brochure, table on p. 6). Busverkehr Grenchen (BGU) has one ([urban-transport-magazine.com](https://www.urban-transport-magazine.com/en/hess-lightram-electric-buses-for-grenchen-and-zurich/)). The gold demonstrator at Busworld 2023 is on [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hess_lighTram_12_Plug,_Busworld_Europe_2023,_Brussels_(P1140382).jpg). Its nose looks the same, so check the length and the number of windows between the axles.
> - **VBZ's lighTram trolleybuses** (lighTram 3/4/5, lighTram 19 DC 200–208, lighTram 25 DC 1–13) share the name and styling. They are articulated trolleybuses with poles. Some Flickr and Commons titles just say "VBZ lighTram", so check for an articulation joint.
> - **Other lighTram 10 operators:** I found none I could verify. The search engine was down for most of this session, so this is open.
> - **Other VBZ midibuses and Quartierbusse.** Wikipedia's fleet table ([DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkehrsbetriebe_Z%C3%BCrich), checked 2026-11-25) still lists **4 MAN Lion's City M (A35) from 331–338** (2011), which have been retiring since 2024. That makes 19 VBZ midibuses with the 15 Hess, plus no. 184. I could not check the "18 Quartierbusse" figure from the 2025 VBZ facts. Earlier Quartierbus types are all retired: Neoplan N 4009 (301–309), Fiat Ducato (314–321), Mercedes Sprinter 616 CDI (322–327) and Renault Master (331–359). **The Renault Masters used the same numbers 340–349**, so a "VBZ 345" in old photos or archives (e.g. an old Saurer 4ZP from the ETH-Bibliothek archive on Commons) is not this bus.

> [!info] Fleet status (checked 2026-11-25)
> - **All 15 are in service.** Wikipedia's fleet table lists 340–347 (2022, 8 of 8 still running) and 348–354 (2023, 7 of 7), with no retirements. I found no reports of new deliveries or a follow-up order. The [VBZ page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/batteriebusse.html) still says "2022, 15 units" and is partly out of date.
> - **Lines:** In 2022 the buses started on 35, 38, 39, 73 and some runs on 40 ([vbzonline.ch](https://vbzonline.ch/aufgeladen-und-ausgedieselt/)). In 2024 Hess named 35, 38, 39, 40, 42, 64 and 73 for the type. Wikipedia's 2026 Quartierbus table lists **38, 39, 40, 42, 45, 64 and 73**, all run with midibuses. It does not list line 35, and I couldn't confirm why. The same page lists the night line **N6** as run with a "Batterie-Midibus". Photo evidence: 349 on line 39 (Sep 2024, photo-01).
> - The Swiss eBus plus (no. 184) runs alongside them on the same lines until the project ends in 2026. I found nothing on what happens to it after that.

## Game and sim assets

### Bus Simulator 27 (paid, officially licensed): the only lighTram 10 Plug model I found
- [Bus Simulator 27](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2397320/Bus_Simulator_27/) came out on **8 Sep 2026**. It costs **CHF 26.75**, and was on sale for CHF 17.65 when I checked. The official Bus Simulator Facebook page promoted it during pre-order with a clip captioned "Let's hit the road with our brand-new HESS lighTram 10 Plug!" ([post, Aug 2026](https://www.facebook.com/BusSimulatorGame/videos/bus-simulator-27-hess-clip/2558100701305476/)). There is also a [19-minute gameplay video of the bus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBrAX-lytT4).
- It seems to be in the **base game**. None of the four Steam DLCs lists it. The **Deluxe Pack** (CHF 4.15) adds the **lighTram 12 Plug** instead.
- Caveats: the game is set in the fictional "Felicia Bay", so the bus probably comes in a generic or fictional livery. I did not check whether there is a VBZ skin. The mesh is locked in an Unreal Engine 5 game under its EULA. Use it only to look at the shape and interior in-game. It does show that a licensed model of this exact type exists.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Put `VBZ-lighTram10-press-01-side.jpg` in Blender as the side backdrop and scale it so the body is 10.80 m. Check that the wheels land about 4.8 m apart. Overlay `VBZ-lighTram10-Hess-side-sketch.png` and line its wheels up with the photo. Don't use its length arrow, because the rear is cut off. Use 2.55 × 3.30 m for the cross-section.
> 2. Block out the nose from press-02 (head-on) and photo-02/03 (3/4). The front mask, the windscreen wrapping into the roof dome and the lamp clusters are the hard part. The renders (press-04/05) show the surfaces most cleanly.
> 3. Model the rear from press-05. Take the roof boxes from the sketch and photo-01. Model the door side from press-01/03/08 and the left side from photo-01 and press-06.
> 4. Pick a version early. For 340–354, use the white roof dome and no "Swiss eBus plus" lettering. For no. 184, use the blue roof dome and the lettering. The VBZ livery is white above and blue below, with a thin blue line under the roof edge.
