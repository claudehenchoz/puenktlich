# VBZ Hess Swisstrolley 4 (BGT-N2D, 162–182) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Hess Swisstrolley 4** is the fourth generation of the Hess low-floor articulated trolleybus, built since 2012 as **BGT-N2D** (two driven axles). VBZ has **21 of them, fleet numbers 162–182**, delivered between March and September 2013. They came in one order with 12 lighTram double-articulated trolleybuses: 33 vehicles worth about CHF 45 million. They were the first VBZ trolleybuses with an electric store (a traction battery) instead of a diesel unit for emergency running. Hess built the chassis and body as general contractor, and Vossloh Kiepe supplied the 600 V electrics. Sources: [VBZ Trolleybusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/trolleybusse.html), the 2011 Hess press clipping saved below, and [Wikipedia (DE) – Hess Swisstrolley](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess_Swisstrolley#Swisstrolley_4). This note is part of [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The predecessor is the [[VBZ Hess Swisstrolley 3 - 3D Models and Modelling References|Swisstrolley 3 (144–161)]], the double-articulated sister from the same order is the [[VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus - 3D Models and Modelling References|lighTram]], and the newer single-articulated type is the [[VBZ Hess lighTram 19 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References|lighTram 19 DC]].

> [!important] Fleet status (checked 2026-11-25): all 21 in service
> [transphoto.org – VBZ trolleybuses](https://transphoto.org/list.php?did=408) lists all of 162–182 as active, with arrival dates from 08.03.2013 (162) to 06.09.2013 (181, 182), and none withdrawn. VBZ's page and [Wikipedia (DE) – Trolleybus Zürich](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybus_Z%C3%BCrich) also list 21. The 2025 retirements hit only the older Swisstrolley 3 (10 of 18 gone, see that note).

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Swisstrolley 3 (VBZ 144–161)** has the same CO-BOLT 3 body and front. Wikipedia says the first ST4 with the normal front kept the old exterior design "apart from small adjustments". In photos, tell them apart by the **fleet number** and the **destination display**: **orange LED** on the ST4 (photo-01, 02; the 2013 VBZ page also says "LED-Anzeigen"), **green-yellow** on the ST3. See the crop below.
> - **Hidden differences** (Wikipedia): ZF independent front suspension on axle 1, stronger drive axles that allow a wider aisle between the wheel arches, a lighter modular chassis, and LED interior lighting. VBZ figures: ST4 2 × 120 kW with a 75 kW battery, ST3 2 × 160 kW with a 120 kW battery. Both have **Kiepe** electrics. I could not see the front-suspension difference in any photo.
> - **Lausanne TL 866–892** (2012–2013) are ST4 of the same era and the closest free photo source (photo-03, 04, 06). **Lucerne VBL 227–230** (2016) are later ST4 (photo-05). **Limoges 901–904** have an optional tram-like front and covered wheel hubs. **Arnhem 5243–5273** are three-door versions with only axle 3 driven.
> - **Type-code trap:** VBZ and Wikipedia also call the **lighTram 19 DC (200–236)** "BGT-N2D", and VBZ lists it as "Hess Swisstrolley5 BGT-N2D". That is the fifth generation with a new front and a roof battery. Don't mix the two up when searching.
> - **SwissTrolley plus (183)**, 2016, is the fifth-generation prototype with the new front.

## Free game assets (closest thing to a download)

### Transport Fever 2: Seamon's Hess Swisstrolley
Seamon's pack covers **all versions of the Swisstrolley 3 and 4 with the CO-BOLT 3 look** and the lighTram. Variants differ in length (18 / 18.75 / 24.25 m), door count and first-door type, A/C size and trolley poles. It has 3 LODs, animated doors and wipers, and an interior. Repaints that need the original are free; stand-alone repaints and ports need Seamon's consent (CC BY-NC after 6 months without an answer). The TF2 page does not list liveries, so I could not confirm a VBZ ST4. The meshes need the game.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Build the shared body once, following the ST3 note (3D Warehouse GLB for volumes, OMSI lighTram 3 source for the CO-BOLT 3 front). Keep the single-leaf front door.
> 2. Scale photo-05 (VBL 227, offside) to 18.7 m as the side backdrop. Check the window and door positions against photo-04 (TL 874) and the VBZ ST3 door-side photos.
> 3. Model the front from photo-02 (head-on) and photo-01 / photo-03 (3/4). Use photo-07 only for the interior shell, not the fittings.
> 4. Make the ST4 differences: orange LED destination display, fleet number 162–182. If you also want an ST3, swap in the green-yellow display and a number from 144–161.
