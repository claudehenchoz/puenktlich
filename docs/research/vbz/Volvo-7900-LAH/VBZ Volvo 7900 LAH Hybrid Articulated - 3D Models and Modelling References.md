# VBZ Volvo 7900 LAH Hybrid Articulated – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Volvo 7900 Hybrid Articulated** (VBZ type name *7900 LAH*, the "LAH" is Volvo's code for the low-floor articulated hybrid) is an 18 m, three-axle, four-door city bus with a parallel hybrid drive: a Volvo D5K 240 four-cylinder diesel (177 kW) plus a Volvo I-SAM electric motor (150 kW) and a lithium-ion battery. VBZ lists it at **18.13 × 2.55 × 3.28 m**, 28 t, 107 places (43 seats). It is part of the [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview|VBZ bus fleet]]. The 2020 batch is longer and has its own note: [[VBZ Volvo 7900 HA Hybrid Articulated - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 HA]]. The 12 m plug-in sister is [[VBZ Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid - 3D Models and Modelling References|VBZ Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid]].

**Fleet numbers (resolved).** The [VBZ Hybridbusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/hybridbusse.html) says "19 units" but gives the numbers as "440–448 (+1 ex-test bus 190)". That is only 10 numbers, so the field is a typo. The correct range is **190 + 440–457 = 19 buses**:
- [6-tram.ch – Gelenkautobusse](https://6-tram.ch/rund-um-die-vbz/fahrzeuge/gelenkautobusse/) lists "190, 440–457, 19 units, 2015 (190) / 2017, Volvo 7900 LAH".
- bus-bild.de has dated photos of 443, 445, 446, 447, 448, 450, 452 and 455 (ZH 608455), so numbers above 448 exist.
- The VBZ annual reports give the delivery history: the hybrid bus "in service since March 2015" (190) plus 13 more in 2017 ([GB 2017](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2017.pdf)), then "5 more hybrid articulated buses in 2018, the fleet now counts 19" ([GB 2018](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/stzh/vbz/Deutsch/Ueber%20das%20Departement/Dokumente/dievbz/geschaeftsbericht/archiv-gb-und-fakten/VBZ_GB_2018.pdf)). So the "2017" series really spans 2017–2018. Which numbers came in 2018 is not published; 453–457 is a guess.
- [ÖV Panorama (Nov 2024)](https://www.ovpanorama.ch/index.php/2024/11/12/drei-jahrzehnte-polnische-schweden/) counts 34 classic hybrid articulated Volvos at VBZ. That is 19 LAH + 15 HA.

**Status (Nov 2026): in service.** The newest dated fan photo I found is 450 on line 61 on 18 Oct 2025 ([bus-bild.de](https://www.bus-bild.de/name/galerie/kategorie/schweiz~betriebe~verkehrsbetriebe-zurich-vbz.html)). No withdrawals are reported. A Hungarian trade site wrote in July 2026 that VBZ runs "32 Volvo 7900A Hybrid" (only seen as a search snippet of [magyarbusz.info](https://magyarbusz.info/en/2026/07/29/zurich-electric-bus-tender-concluded-yutong-and-daimler-buses-replace-the-hess-man-pairing/); the page is behind a bot wall). That would mean two of the 34 are gone. Not verified. VBZ's July 2026 order for Yutong and eCitaro G e-buses (see [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview|the hub note]]) does not name the hybrids as the buses to be replaced.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **VBZ 7900 HA (458–472, 2020)** has the **same body generation and the same face**. Volvo's Swiss rescue sheets show both lengths with "Produktionsstart 2014". The HA is the **18.7 m** length option: the extra ~0.6 m sits in the **rear section between the articulation and the rear axle** (overlay of the two Volvo side views in the HA note). Front section, doors and front axle are identical. Other tells: the HA came with **white LED destination displays**; the LAH came with **orange LED** ones (photo busbild-447, busbild-190), but some LAH have since been fitted with white displays (busbild-455), so don't rely on the display alone. Check the fleet number.
> - **190** is the 2015 demonstrator/test bus. In the 2023 photo (busbild-190) it looks the same as the 2017 buses: same livery, orange display, "HYBRID" lettering.
> - **Other Swiss 7900A Hybrids:** Bernmobil 871–891 (2016, orange displays, classic mirrors) and Bernmobil's 2022 series (white displays, **camera mirrors**; see the HA note), VBL Luzern 162 (photo-10). Same body, different paint and mirrors.
> - **Volvo 7900 Electric / Electric Articulated** (full battery bus): same body family but no exhaust, battery pods on the roof and a different rear. Not the same.
> - **Volvo 7900 Electric Hybrid** (12 m plug-in, VBZ 620–635): 2 axles, roof charging pod.
> - **Volvo 7700A / 7000A** (older generation) and the **Volvo BZL** (new chassis with third-party bodies such as MCV) look different.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Load `VBZ-Volvo7900LAH-Volvo-brochure-2013-drawing-18044.png` in Blender and scale the plan to 18,044 mm (VBZ quotes 18.13 m; the difference is probably bumpers or mirrors). Use 2,550 × 3,280 mm for the cross-section.
> 2. If the CC BY Sketchfab *2014 Volvo 7900A Hybrid* is good enough, import it, check it against the drawing and repaint it. Otherwise block out the body from the drawing and use the Sketchfab mesh or the CS1 D3S model only to check volumes.
> 3. Front: photo-01, photo-07, photo-08 and busbild-447/455. Rear: photo-09 and photo-12. Door side: photo-04, photo-05 and busbild-455. Roof equipment: the rescue sheet.
> 4. Livery (from the photos): white body, blue lower band, dark glazing band, a black panel behind the windscreen around the front door with "HYBRID" lettering at the top, and the "VBZ Züri-Linie" logo on the front and sides. Pick the display colour by fleet number (orange as delivered).
