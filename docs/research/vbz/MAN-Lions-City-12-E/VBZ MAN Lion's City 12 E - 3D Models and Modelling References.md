# VBZ MAN Lion's City 12 E (12CE, 636–657) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **MAN Lion's City 12 E** is the 12 m battery-electric version of MAN's 2018 city bus generation. MAN's type code is **NL 367**, and VBZ calls it **"Lion's City 12CE"**. VBZ has **22 of them, fleet numbers 636–657**. The first 15 were ordered in July 2021 and delivered in 2022 (636–650). Seven more followed in 2023 (651–657) to replace some Mercedes O 530 Citaros ([Wikipedia DE, VBZ fleet table](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkehrsbetriebe_Z%C3%BCrich), [TIR transNews, July 2021](https://www.tir-transnews.ch/bus/15-man-lions-city-12-e-fuer-die-stadt-zuerich/)). They have 3 doors and a 480 kWh roof battery. They charge overnight at the Hardau garage with CCS plugs at 100 or 150 kW. They first ran on lines 66, 77, 78 and 99. This note belongs to [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The articulated sister is [[VBZ MAN Lion's City 18 E - 3D Models and Modelling References]], and VBZ's older diesel articulated MANs are in [[VBZ MAN Lion's City G - 3D Models and Modelling References]].

**Status (November 2026): in service, but no more will be bought.** Bus-bild.de has photos of 637 (line 66), 638 (line 185) and 654 (line 184, Adliswil) taken on 18 November 2026 ([bus-bild.de VBZ gallery](https://www.bus-bild.de/name/galerie/kategorie/schweiz~betriebe~verkehrsbetriebe-zurich-vbz.html)). On 29 July 2026 the city said the electric buses bought in 2022/2023 were of **"mangelhafte Qualität"** (poor quality). VBZ will not take up the remaining MAN options. Instead it is buying 8 standard e-buses from Yutong France and 12 articulated e-buses from Daimler Buses Schweiz for about CHF 17 million, to run from mid-2027 ([Stadt Zürich press release](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/de/aktuell/medienmitteilungen/2026/07/gruenes-licht-fuer-beschaffung-neuer-elektrobusse.html)). The press named heating, ventilation and doors as the problems, plus late deliveries ([Tages-Anzeiger, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.tagesanzeiger.ch/vbz-kaufen-20-elektrobusse-bei-yutong-und-daimler-buses-739779690986), [Tsüri, 29 Jul 2026](https://tsri.ch/a/nach-maengeln-an-bestehender-flotte-stadt-kauft-20-neue-elektrobusse)). In the summer heat, battery charging was cut off to protect the batteries, and some air conditioners failed. Buses with those faults were kept off the road ([watson, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.watson.ch/schweiz/zuerich/690349878-vbz-reagiert-auf-sicherheitsbedenken-bei-den-chinesischen-yutong-bussen)). Tsüri says the buses stay in service once the faults are fixed.

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Diesel and hybrid Lion's City 12 (12C, EfficientHybrid):** same body, front and side windows. They have an **engine tower** in a rear corner, a rear engine grille and an exhaust. The E has **no engine tower**, so the rear corner is clear and MAN fits a full-width rear bench there. Its roof carries **long battery packs**, the heat-pump AC unit, inverters and a battery cooler (see the MAN cutaway below). Most free game assets (REV0 "MAN Lion's City 2018", the TF2 mod, OMSI 2 add-on) are the diesel version.
> - **Lion's City 12 E LE** (low entry, shown in 2023) and the **10 E** (10.5 m) are different lengths and floor layouts. The **18 E** is the articulated sister (VBZ 474–503).
> - **Same model, other Swiss operators:** TL Lausanne runs Lion's City 12 E buses (photo-13 and [Commons search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=%22Lion%27s+City+E%22+Schweiz&ns6=1)). Rietze makes 10 E models for Margarethen Bus (Basel) and Busland BLS.
> - **Contractor buses in VBZ colours:** per the task brief, ATE Bus and Limmat Bus run diesel and hybrid Lion's City buses for VBZ. I did not verify their types. Bus-bild.de shows older third-generation Lion's City buses in VBZ livery at Schwerzenbach in July 2026 ([example](https://www.bus-bild.de/bild/schweiz~betriebe~verkehrsbetriebe-zurich-vbz/226642/vbz---man-lions-city-hybrid.html)). These are a different body.
> - **Model year 2024 onward:** MAN moved all Lion's City models to a new electronics platform with a 12-inch digital dashboard ([simulatoren-news](https://simulatoren-news.com/article/1498-gro%C3%9Fes-update-der-man-lion-s-city-e-h%C3%A4lt-einzug-in-omsi-2/)). The VBZ buses (2022/2023) have the earlier cab.
> - **Data mismatch:** the current MAN brochure gives 12,185 mm × 3,300 mm. VBZ and MAN's 2020 brochure give 12,200 mm × 3,320 mm. Use the VBZ figures.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Put `VBZ-MANLC12E-MAN-drawing-views.png` in Blender as a background. Use the **row-1 side view (3 doors)** and scale it to 12,200 mm. Use 2,550 × 3,320 mm for the cross-section, and the front and rear views from the same sheet.
> 2. To start from a mesh, download KarpWorkz's CC BY "Lion's City 12G" from Sketchfab for the 2018 body shell. Delete the engine tower and close the rear with a full-height window (photo-01/11/12). For a game-ready check, own Cities: Skylines and open Fishyfish's 3-door 12 E.
> 3. Model the roof from the MAN cutaway and the Rietze 76333 photo. In the cutaway the front is at the bottom right. From the rear: two battery packs above the rear axle, the heat-pump AC unit, the inverter and battery cooler, then four battery packs over the front half.
> 4. Do the front from photo-04/05/06 and the doors from photo-07/13. Finish with the VBZ livery from the rendering: white, blue skirt, "VBZ Züri-Linie" logo and black glazing band.
