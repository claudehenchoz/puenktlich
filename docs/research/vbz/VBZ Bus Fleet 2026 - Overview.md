# VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 – Overview

This is the index of the bus models in the fleet of the **Verkehrsbetriebe Zürich (VBZ)** that are in service as of November 2026. Each model has its own research note covering 3D models, game assets, drawings and photos. They are built the same way as the train notes ([[SBB RABe 511 - 3D Models and Modelling References|RABe 511]], [[SBB RABe 512 - 3D Models and Modelling References|RABe 512]]).

At the end of 2025 VBZ had **260 buses**, all low-floor: 35 double-articulated trolleybuses, 53 articulated trolleybuses, 48 standard buses, 106 articulated buses and 18 Quartierbusse. Source: [VBZ Fakten 2025 (PDF)](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/vbz/de/die-vbz/zahlen-fakten/geschaeftsbericht/Z4690_VBZ_FB-2025_Web.pdf).

## Not covered
- **Contractor buses in VBZ livery.** ATE Bus and Limmat Bus run MAN Lion's City and Mercedes Citaro C2 / C2 G buses with their own fleet numbers (21–69) on regional lines *for* VBZ. They aren't VBZ's own fleet, so they appear only as look-alikes in the MAN and Citaro notes. Source: [busphoto.eu fleet list](https://busphoto.eu/show.php?grid=511).
- **Retired:** the Neoplan Centroliner N4522 articulated buses (2003–2006). The Tram-Museum ran farewell trips in 2023 and expected them to be gone by the end of 2023 ([tram-museum.ch](https://www.tram-museum.ch/agenda/abschied-bus-generation-neoplan-231021)). The [VBZ Autobusse page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/autobusse.html) still lists them, but that page is out of date. The Neoplan standard buses and the Mercedes O 405 GTZ trolleybuses (last ones 2015) are also retired.
- **On order:** in July 2026 VBZ ordered **8 Yutong 12 m e-buses** (via Yutong France) and **12 Mercedes-Benz eCitaro G** (Daimler Buses Schweiz), for delivery by mid-2027. There are options for 20 more standard and 60 more articulated buses. VBZ said it was dissatisfied with the quality of its Hess and MAN e-buses. Sources: [Stadt Zürich press release](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/de/aktuell/medienmitteilungen/2026/07/gruenes-licht-fuer-beschaffung-neuer-elektrobusse.html), [mobilityblog.ch](https://www.mobilityblog.ch/vbz-beschafft-elektrobusse/), [tsri.ch](https://tsri.ch/a/nach-maengeln-an-bestehender-flotte-stadt-kauft-20-neue-elektrobusse).
