# VBZ Hess lighTram 25 DC (double-articulated battery trolleybus, fleet 1–13) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **Hess lighTram® 25 DC** is a 24.7 m, four-axle, double-articulated **battery trolleybus**. "DC" stands for Hess's *Dynamic Charging*: the bus charges its traction battery from the overhead wires while it runs and covers sections without wires on battery. In November 2022 VBZ gave Hess an order worth about CHF 330 million. The first batch was **13 lighTram 19 DC** and **13 lighTram 25 DC**, with options for 85 more 19 DC and 55 more 25 DC ([Urban Transport Magazine, 17 Nov 2022](https://www.urban-transport-magazine.com/grossauftrag-fuer-hess-lightram-gehen-nach-zuerich/)). The double-articulated buses are numbered **1–13** and were built in 2025/26. German Wikipedia gives the type as **BGGT-N2** with **ABB** electrics (English Wikipedia says Kiepe). They are meant for lines 31 and 32 and for converting line 80 to electric operation ([Wikipedia DE – Hess lighTram Trolley](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess_lighTram_Trolley)). Hess designed a **new VBZ look** for this order, with large standing areas at doors 2, 3 and 4. The forerunner is the 2018 **Doppelgelenk-Trolleybus plus (92–94)**, which has its own section below. This note belongs to [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The articulated sister is [[VBZ Hess lighTram 19 DC - 3D Models and Modelling References]], and the older double-articulated fleet is in [[VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus - 3D Models and Modelling References]].

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **I found no photo of a real VBZ 1–13.** Commons has none (category and full-text search, 2026-11-25), and web search returned nothing usable. The only images of the VBZ design are the **Hess design renders from 2022** (saved below). The delivered buses may differ in detail. Check a real photo before you commit to the front.
> - **VBZ 92–94** (2018) is a **different, older body**: a 5th-generation Hess front with a white roof cowling and the destination display behind the top of a large curved windscreen (photo-01 to 03 below). Do not mix it with 1–13.
> - **VBZ 61–91** (lighTram 3 / 4, 2007–2014) has the older rounded VBZ front. See [[VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus - 3D Models and Modelling References]].
> - **Other lighTram 25 DC (lighTram 5 generation):** St. Gallen VBSG 131–143 (2020/2024, red and white, look-alike-01 to 03), Bern 41–54 (2018/19) and 55–67 (2026), Lausanne TL 701–712 (2020) and TL 727 (look-alike-04), Lucerne VBL 418–422 (2019), Winterthur 401–411 (2022) and Nancy (25 buses from 2024). Source: Wikipedia DE. These share the 24.7 m platform and the axle layout, but not the VBZ front.
> - **Battery-only versions without poles:** lighTram 25 TOSA in Brisbane (look-alike-05, 06) and Nantes, and the Geneva lighTram 19 TOSA. Brisbane's dark glazing band and wrap-around windscreen look closer to the VBZ render than the St. Gallen front does. That is my visual impression only; I did not confirm that the body is the same.
> - **Hess lighTram 25 OPP** (Basel) charges at stops with a pantograph and has no trolley poles.

## Free game assets (closest thing to a download)
None of these is the VBZ 1–13. All need the game, and the meshes are in game formats.

### Transport Fever 3 (mod.io): St. Gallen lighTram 25 DC (best shape match)
- [VBSG HESS lighTram 19 & 25 DC](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/vbsg-hess-lightram-19-25-dc) by AlbertSchweizer, live since **16 Nov 2026**, version 1.0. When I checked it had **2,406 subscribers** and 2,613 downloads, and the PC file is 2.62 MB. It has St. Gallen lighTram 25 DC **no. 134** (and lighTram 19 DC no. 102) in VBSG red and white, with interiors, moving joints, animated doors and night lighting. The author says the models were made "using HESS documentation and photos of the VBSG fleet". It is the right platform (lighTram 5, 24.7 m, four axles) but the St. Gallen front, not the new VBZ front.

### Predecessor: Doppelgelenk-Trolleybus plus / lighTram plus (92–94, 2018)
After lines 31 and 34 were joined in December 2017, VBZ needed more double-articulated buses and called off three from its options ([6-tram.ch](https://6-tram.ch/rund-um-die-vbz/fahrzeuge/trolleybusse/)). They have the same technology as the single *SwissTrolley plus* no. 183, but a bigger **70 kWh** battery, and have run on lines 31 and 32 since **September 2018** ([vbzonline.ch](https://vbzonline.ch/happy-birthday-trolleybus/)). Wikipedia DE counts them as **lighTram 5** with ABB electrics. They are the first VBZ double-articulated buses with the 5th-generation Hess front, so the lighTram 25 DC assets above fit them better than they fit 1–13.

## Fleet status (checked 2026-11-25)
- **Ordered:** November 2022 ([Urban Transport Magazine](https://www.urban-transport-magazine.com/grossauftrag-fuer-hess-lightram-gehen-nach-zuerich/)). The plan then was service "from 2024".
- **Delivered:** delivery of the double-articulated buses (from no. 1) began in **mid-2025** ([Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybuses_in_Zurich), citing *Trolleybus Magazine* 384). Wikipedia DE lists **1–13, built 2025/2026**. [VBZ Fakten 2025](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/content/dam/vbz/de/die-vbz/zahlen-fakten/geschaeftsbericht/Z4690_VBZ_FB-2025_Web.pdf) gives 35 double-articulated trolleybuses at the end of 2025, against 34 before. So only part of the order was in service then, or old buses had already been retired (the PDF does not say which).
- **Delays:** in January 2025 VBZ reported delivery delays and quality defects with new electric buses from MAN and Hess and had to hire in used buses ([bahnonline.ch, 16 Jan 2025](https://www.bahnonline.ch/72674/fremdfahrzeuge-im-vbz-gebiet-unterwegs/)). Trolleybus service on line 69 started only on 19 October 2026 "because further trolleybuses were delivered late" (Wikipedia DE).
- **Effect on 61–77:** Wikipedia DE says that from 2025 several lighTram 3 were replaced by new lighTrams and retired, without numbers. I could not confirm which. See [[VBZ Hess lighTram 3 Double-Articulated Trolleybus - 3D Models and Modelling References]].
- **92–94** are still listed on the VBZ and Wikipedia pages.
- The [VBZ trolleybus page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/trolleybusse.html) has no entry for the lighTram 25 DC yet.

> [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Decide first: **1–13** (new VBZ design, renders only) or **92–94** (5th-gen front, real photos). For 1–13 the front is a guess until real photos exist, so block it out loosely.
> 2. Set up a 24.7 × 2.55 × 3.5 m box. Take the axle layout (axles 1 + 4 steered, 2 + 3 driven) and the section and joint proportions from the St. Gallen profile (look-alike-01). Scale it to 24.7 m and measure from it.
> 3. Model the joints, wheels, roof battery and pole housings from look-alike-01 to 04, and the cab and interior from look-alike-07 to 09.
> 4. Shape the front and the livery from the Hess render (1–13) or from plus93-photo-01 to 03 (92–94). For 1–13, compare with Brisbane (look-alike-05, 06) for the dark glazing band.
> 5. If you own Transport Fever 3, open the VBSG lighTram 25 DC mod to check proportions. For Cities: Skylines, REV0's bi-articulated trolley is a rough lighTram 5 volume.
