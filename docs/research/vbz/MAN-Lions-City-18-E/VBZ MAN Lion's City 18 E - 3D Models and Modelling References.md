# VBZ MAN Lion's City 18 E (18CE, 474–503) – 3D Models and Modelling References

The **MAN Lion's City 18 E** is the 18 m articulated battery-electric version of MAN's 2018 city bus generation. MAN's type code is **NG 734**, and VBZ calls it **"Lion's City 18CE"**. VBZ has **28 of them** according to the [Wikipedia DE fleet table](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkehrsbetriebe_Z%C3%BCrich): **500–503** (4 buses, listed as 2022), **474–494** (21 buses, 2023) and **495–497** (3 buses, 2024). The 21 from 2023 were called off from the MAN options in December 2021 and replaced the last Neoplan Centroliner N 4522s. The 3 from 2024 were ordered in March 2023 to enlarge the fleet. The [VBZ page](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/vbz/de/die-vbz/fahrzeuge/busse/batteriebusse.html) still says "Baujahr 2022, Stückzahl 22", but it lists both number ranges (28 buses), so that count is out of date. The buses have 3 doors and a 640 kWh roof battery, and charge overnight with CCS plugs at 100 or 150 kW. This note belongs to [[VBZ Bus Fleet 2026 - Overview]]. The solo sister is [[VBZ MAN Lion's City 12 E - 3D Models and Modelling References]], and VBZ's older diesel articulated MANs are in [[VBZ MAN Lion's City G - 3D Models and Modelling References]].

**Status (November 2026): in service, but no more will be bought.** Bus-bild.de has 494 on line 70 on 18 November 2026, and 474, 476 and 493 on lines 304, 161 and 165 in late 2024 ([bus-bild.de VBZ gallery](https://www.bus-bild.de/name/galerie/kategorie/schweiz~betriebe~verkehrsbetriebe-zurich-vbz.html)). Wikipedia lists the night lines N7 and N91 as run with battery articulated buses. On 29 July 2026 the city said the electric buses bought in 2022/2023 were of **"mangelhafte Qualität"** (poor quality). VBZ will not take up the remaining MAN options (there were options for up to 80 articulated e-buses). Instead it is buying 8 standard e-buses from Yutong France and **12 articulated e-buses from Daimler Buses Schweiz** for about CHF 17 million, to run from mid-2027 ([Stadt Zürich press release](https://www.stadt-zuerich.ch/de/aktuell/medienmitteilungen/2026/07/gruenes-licht-fuer-beschaffung-neuer-elektrobusse.html)). The release does not name the Daimler model; it will presumably be a Mercedes eCitaro G. The press named heating, ventilation and doors as the problems, plus late deliveries ([Tages-Anzeiger, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.tagesanzeiger.ch/vbz-kaufen-20-elektrobusse-bei-yutong-und-daimler-buses-739779690986), [Tsüri, 29 Jul 2026](https://tsri.ch/a/nach-maengeln-an-bestehender-flotte-stadt-kauft-20-neue-elektrobusse)). In the summer heat, battery charging was cut off to protect the batteries, and some air conditioners failed. Buses with those faults were kept off the road ([watson, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.watson.ch/schweiz/zuerich/690349878-vbz-reagiert-auf-sicherheitsbedenken-bei-den-chinesischen-yutong-bussen)).

> [!warning] Look-alikes and variants
> - **Diesel, CNG and hybrid Lion's City 18 (18C, 18G):** same body and windows. They have an **engine tower** and engine grille in the rear section, and a single driven axle. The E has **two driven axles** (central motors on axles 2 and 3), **no engine tower**, and battery packs and the AC unit on the roof of both sections. Most free game assets (REV0 "MAN Lion's City 2018 18-4", the TF2 mod, the OMSI 2 add-on) are the diesel version.
> - **Door count:** VBZ's 18CE has **3 doors**. Many other operators (Hochbahn, many German cities) have **4 doors**, and so does the D3S Cities: Skylines asset. The MAN drawings below show both. Check which one a photo shows before you copy door positions.
> - **VBZ's older MAN Lion's City GL (A40, 510–515, 2015)** is the previous generation. See [[VBZ MAN Lion's City G - 3D Models and Modelling References]].
> - **Swiss look-alikes:** Lion's City E buses also run for TL Lausanne and BOGG Olten (see [bus-bild.de](https://www.bus-bild.de/name/galerie/kategorie/alternative-antriebe~elektrobus-vollelektrisch~man-niederflurbus-4-generation-new-lions-city.html)). Per the task brief, ATE Bus and Limmat Bus run diesel and hybrid Lion's City buses for VBZ. I did not verify their types.
> - **Data mismatch:** VBZ and MAN's 2020 brochure give 18,100 × 3,320 mm. MAN's current datasheet gives 18,100 × 3,300 mm (max), and the current brochure gives 18,060 mm. That matches the sum of the datasheet's overhangs and wheelbases (2,775 + 5,200 + 6,680 + 3,405 = 18,060 mm). Use 18,060 mm for the body and the axle positions.

> - **Sketchfab** (search API; queries "lion's city e", "lions city e", "lion's city 18", "lions city 18", "lion's city g", "lions city gelenkbus", "man lion's city articulated", "man articulated bus", "gelenkbus", "man 18c", "electric bus 18m", "e-bus", "man lions city", "man bus", "vbz", "zürich bus"): no articulated 2018-generation Lion's City at all. > [!tip] Suggested modelling workflow
> 1. Put `VBZ-MANLC18E-MAN-drawing-views.png` in Blender as a background and use **row 3 (3-door right side)** and row 4 (left side). Scale them to 18,060 mm. Place the axles from the datasheet: front overhang 2,775 mm, then 5,200 mm, then 6,680 mm, rear overhang 3,405 mm. Use 2,550 × 3,320 mm for the cross-section.
> 2. Use `VBZ-MANLC18E-MAN-datasheet-side.png` for door, window and wheel-arch shapes, and photo-01 to check the left side and roof line.
> 3. If you build the 12 E first (see that note), reuse its front and rear modules and its roof layout. Then add the joint and bellows (photo-01/09) and extend the roof battery and AC fairings over both sections (Rietze 76500).
> 4. For a game-ready check, own Cities: Skylines and open Fishyfish's 3-door 18 E. Finish with the VBZ livery (white, blue skirt, "VBZ Züri-Linie", black glazing band).
